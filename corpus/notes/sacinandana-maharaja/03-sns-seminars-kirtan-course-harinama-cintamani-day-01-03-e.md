# 03 SNS Seminars Kirtan Course Harinama Cintamani Day 01 03 Evening Last Session ISKCON Radhadesh

## Theme

This evening session frames the Kīrtan course as far more than an educational program — it is a heart-purification program. Its arc moves from practice to prayer to transformation. Sacīnandana Mahārāja teaches that sustained saṅkīrtana — the evening kīrtana offered for the pleasure of Rādhā-Gopīnātha — is itself transformative, assuring that after two months of regular practice the devotee 'will not be the same.' But the engine of transformation is prayer: he repeatedly commends prayer as essential to purify the heart and awaken taste for the holy name, holding up Haridāsa Ṭhākura's prayer to Lord Caitanya as the model — 'keep me in this world if You wish, but feed me with the taste, the nectar, of the holy name.' The central principle is that deeper taste (ruci) for the holy name is not manufactured by effort but arises automatically as the heart becomes purified. Hence the devotee's part is to keep practicing and keep praying for that purification, trusting the taste to follow.

## Practical application

Approach daily japa and Hari-Nāma not merely as a discipline to master but as a means of purifying the heart, and expect that the taste will come as a by-product of that purification rather than something to be forced. Make prayer a fixed companion of chanting: before or during japa, pray in the mood of Haridāsa Ṭhākura — asking the Lord to feed you the nectar of the holy name and to purify your heart — rather than praying for the removal of material distress. Commit to sustained, regular practice, especially evening kīrtana offered for the pleasure of the Lord (Rādhā-Gopīnātha), trusting that consistency over weeks and months reshapes the practitioner from within. When taste seems absent, do not despair or strain for it; instead intensify the prayer for purification and keep chanting, letting ruci arise on its own as the heart is cleansed.

## Glossary

- **Hari-Nāma** — The holy name of the Lord; the practice of chanting the names of Kṛṣṇa/Hari.
- **japa** — Soft, individual repetition of the holy name, usually counted on beads.
- **kīrtana / saṅkīrtana** — Congregational chanting and glorification of the holy name; saṅkīrtana emphasizes the collective, shared practice.
- **ruci** — Taste or relish for the holy name; a stage of devotional attraction that arises spontaneously as the heart is purified.
- **Haridāsa Ṭhākura** — The great associate of Lord Caitanya honored as the ācārya (exemplar) of the holy name, whose prayer for the nectar of the name is the model held up in this lecture.
- **Lord Caitanya** — Caitanya Mahāprabhu, regarded as the incarnation who inaugurated and exemplified the yuga-dharma of congregational chanting of the holy name.
- **Rādhā-Gopīnātha** — The Deity form of the Divine Couple (Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa as Gopīnātha) for whose pleasure the evening kīrtana is offered.
