# SNS Retreat Holy Name Retreat 02 Kirtan Introduction 2022 11 11

## Theme

This introductory session to the Holy Name Retreat sets the orientation for chanting Hari-Nāma. Sacīnandana Mahārāja begins from the goal: chanting aims to build a firm, intimate relationship with the Holy Name, and through the Name with Krishna Himself, because Krishna is personally and fully present in His Name in this world. He illustrates this with the story of a materialistic man taught to chant Rāma's name even carelessly and in unclean places, drawing from it the foundational principle that the Name is non-different from the Named — so the Name deserves respect and attention, never to be treated as ordinary sound. From there he addresses how the skill of chanting is gained: not through theory but through practice itself, just as one learns swimming only by entering the water or speaking only by speaking. He then frames chanting as simultaneously the means and the goal — it is both the sādhana (practice/path, abhidheya) that leads to perfection and the very perfection itself (prayojana), the glorification of Krishna. He closes the introduction with the practical entry point into a chanting session: sit stably, tall and calm; settle the mind with a few deep breaths; and fix the mind on being near Krishna, turning attention toward Him before the first Name is uttered.

## Practical application

Approach each round of japa as an act of relationship-building rather than a quota to complete: remember that Krishna is personally present in the Name, and chant with the respect and attentive presence that reality deserves. Before beginning, prepare the body and mind — sit in a stable, upright, calm posture, take a few deep breaths to settle, and consciously fix the mind on being near Krishna, turning your attention toward Him before the Name begins. Trust that proficiency in chanting comes from the doing itself, so commit to the daily practice without waiting to feel 'ready' or fully understanding it intellectually — you learn to chant by chanting. Finally, hold both dimensions together: your chanting is at once the practice that purifies and leads you toward perfection, and itself already the perfection of glorifying Krishna, so the destination is present within the means.

## Glossary

- **Hari-Nāma** — The Holy Name of Krishna (Hari); the practice of chanting the Lord's names.
- **Holy Name** — Krishna's name, understood to be non-different from Krishna Himself, who is personally present within it.
- **japa** — The meditative, often individual, repetition of the Holy Name, typically counted on beads.
- **sādhana** — Spiritual practice; the disciplined means by which one progresses toward the goal of devotion.
- **abhidheya** — The process or means of devotional practice — here, chanting as the path leading to the goal.
- **prayojana** — The ultimate goal or destination of practice — here, the perfection of chanting: love for and glorification of Krishna.
- **the Name is not different from the Named** — The principle that the Holy Name is identical with Krishna, so chanting brings one into direct contact with Him.
- **Krishna** — The Supreme Person in the Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇava tradition, fully present in His Name.
- **Rāma** — A name and incarnation of the Supreme Lord, invoked in the story of the materialistic man taught to chant.
