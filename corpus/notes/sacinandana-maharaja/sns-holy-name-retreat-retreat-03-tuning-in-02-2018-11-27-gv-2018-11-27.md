# SNS Holy Name Retreat Retreat 03 Tuning In 02 2018 11 27 GV

## Theme

This lecture, given at the opening 'Tuning In' session of a Holy-Name retreat in the valley of Govardhan beside Giriraj, frames the retreat as a deliberate withdrawal from ordinary engagements into a sacred place where, by Giriraj's blessings and inspiration, a devotee can go deeper in Krishna consciousness with a quality of spiritual support unavailable in everyday life. Mahārāja explains that this setting is, in effect, engineered to give nearly every participant the personal sign or hint — subtle or unmistakable — that they need to take their own next step. He then names two supports that make this possible: the saṅga, the community of sincere searchers who are now to travel as one body, and the inner 'receiver mood' of humble openness. The arc moves from the outer condition (a sacred place set apart) through the shared condition (the blessed company of fellow seekers) to the inner condition (a heart opened in receptivity), establishing that genuine realization is received, not seized, and that tuning oneself to receive is the foundation on which the rest of the retreat's Hari-Nāma practice will be built.

## Practical application

Before sitting for japa, a devotee can recreate this same 'tuning in' on a small scale: set aside competing engagements and treat the time and place of chanting as sacred and set apart, even amid daily life. Approach the Holy Name not as something to be grasped or extracted by effort but as a gift to be received — cultivating the 'receiver mood' of humble, open-hearted attentiveness so that whatever sign, taste, or guidance the Name offers can actually land. Stay alert to the subtle hints that come during chanting, trusting that the next step in one's spiritual life is being shown. And lean on saṅga: chant in the company of, or in conscious connection with, other sincere devotees, regarding oneself as part of a community travelling together rather than struggling alone — letting that fellowship become a real support for advancement in the Holy Name.

## Glossary

- **saṅga** — Spiritual association or community; here, the body of sincere seekers gathered for the retreat, regarded as essential support for advancement.
- **Govardhan** — The sacred hill and surrounding valley in the land of Vraja where the retreat is held — a place charged with Krishna's pastimes.
- **Giriraj** — 'King of mountains' — a name of Govardhan Hill, worshipped as non-different from Krishna, whose blessings and inspiration empower the retreat.
- **Krishna consciousness** — Loving God-centered awareness; the deepening absorption in Krishna that the retreat is meant to foster.
- **Hari-Nāma** — The Holy Name of Hari (Krishna); the chanting practice that is the focus of the retreat.
- **japa** — The practice of softly reciting the Holy Name, usually counted on beads, as one's personal daily chanting.
- **receiver mood** — An inner disposition of humble, open-hearted receptivity — the precondition for genuine realization, which is received rather than seized.
