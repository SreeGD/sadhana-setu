# SNS Retreat Holy Name Retreat 03 Guided Japa Session 02 2023 12 04

## Theme

This guided japa session frames the chanting of the holy name as a practice of sincere, attentive effort rather than forced achievement. Sacīnandana Mahārāja sets the tone for the session by establishing two complementary principles: the chanter's responsibility is to offer earnest, best-quality attention to each round, and the inevitable wandering of the mind is not a failure but an occasion to gently renew one's intention. The arc moves from the positive instruction — chant with sincere care — to its protective counterpart — meet distraction without struggle or discouragement, repeatedly bringing awareness back to the resolve to chant. Together these form a single, patient attitude toward Hari-Nāma in which quality flows from attention and perseverance rather than strain.

## Practical application

In daily japa, the devotee approaches each round resolved simply to give sincere, attentive effort, releasing any anxiety about producing a particular result or feeling. When the mind drifts away from the holy name — as it naturally will — the practitioner neither fights the distraction nor grows discouraged by it. Instead, again and again, calmly and without self-criticism, they return their awareness to their intention to chant and re-engage with the sound of the name. Over a session this becomes a steady rhythm of noticing, releasing, and returning, so that wandering attention becomes a doorway back to the holy name rather than a reason to give up.

## Glossary

- **japa** — The meditative, often soft or whispered, repetition of the holy name of God, typically counted on beads; the practice this session guides.
- **Hari-Nāma** — Literally "the name of Hari (the Lord)"; the chanting of the holy name as a devotional practice.
- **holy name** — The sacred name of God chanted in japa, regarded as non-different from the Lord and the focus of the chanter's attention.
