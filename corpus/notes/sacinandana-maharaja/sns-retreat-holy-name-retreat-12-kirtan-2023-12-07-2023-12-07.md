# SNS Retreat Holy Name Retreat 12 Kirtan 2023 12 07

## Theme

Delivered at Ṭer Kadamba during a Holy-Name retreat, this lecture reads the sacred geography of the place as a living teaching on the Name. The very name 'Ṭer Kadamba' — 'ṭer' meaning to call — memorializes Kṛṣṇa grazing the cows and calling each one (Dhabalī, Śyāmalī, Lālī, Kuṅkumī) and each gopī (Lalitā, Viśākhā and the sakhīs) by her own name, showing that the Lord relates to every devotee personally, never as an anonymous mass. The atmosphere of Vraja is uninterrupted kīrtana — 'all-time bhakti' — and through wordplay the līlā becomes instruction: the 'butter' (mākhan) Kṛṣṇa truly wants is the mind (man), the heart-mirror wiped clean by śrī-kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtana, as Jīva Gosvāmī and the Śikṣāṣṭaka teach. Mahārāja grounds this in the non-difference of the holy name, the Deity form, and Bhagavān — all fully spiritual (cid-ānanda) — so that chanting is direct contact with Kṛṣṇa, not a symbol of Him. He honors Ṭer Kadamba as the seat where Rūpa Gosvāmī, whose Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu defines uttamā bhakti, composed his devotional literature; where Jīva compiled Rūpa's poems so the Rūpānuga Vaiṣṇavas could enter a loving relationship with charming Kṛṣṇa; and where the rūpānuga line — Raghunātha Dāsa, Kṛṣṇadāsa Kavirāja — is established as a succession in mood. The heart of the lecture is an extended meditation on Raghunātha Dāsa Gosvāmī's Sva-niyama-daśakam: the resolve not merely to chant but to LOVE the guru, the Gāyatrī, and the Name; exclusive residence in Vraja birth after birth (preferred above Vaikuṇṭha and Dvārakā), yet loyalty that follows Rādhā Herself wherever She goes; unconditional acceptance of Kṛṣṇa whether cruel or gentle; and rādhā-dāsya — shunning those who neglect Rādhā while honoring even the humblest who worship the Divine Couple. The vows culminate in helpless, renounced bhajana at Rādhā-kuṇḍa, with a phala-śruti promising Vraja to whoever hears them with fixed mind. Mahārāja closes with practical instruction for the kīrtana and the day's 64 rounds: chant with body, mind, and heart together; absorb the dhāma into the heart and carry it onward; reside in Vṛndāvana both in body and in mind, taking shelter in humility at the feet of Rūpa Gosvāmī and the dust of the holy place.

## Practical application

Chant with full participation on three levels at once: the physical senses (sing and hear the Name), the mind (remember and think of the Name as you chant, deliberately recalling the holy land of Vṛndāvana — here Ṭer Kadamba — as you sit down for japa), and the heart (actually express something to Kṛṣṇa, chanting with affection, gratitude, and devotion rather than with the voice alone). Treat the Name as Kṛṣṇa Himself, not a symbol — every round is direct, personal contact, and He calls you by name as He called each cow and gopī. Make love, not bare repetition, the goal: following Raghunātha Dāsa's first vow, aim to love the guru, the Gāyatrī, and the holy name. Let the chanting clean the mirror of the heart — offer Him the 'butter' of a purified mind. Use the dhāma even when away from it: reside in Vṛndāvana within the mind, keeping the heart fixed there so the chanting deepens wherever you physically are. Sustain immersive sādhana — a full day of saṅkīrtana, here 64 rounds — as a powerful practice, and join hands so the chanting becomes a unified, loving call. Frame the whole effort in humility: place the hand on the heart and offer a closing prayer to the dust of the holy place and to Śrīla Rūpa Gosvāmī, recognizing that the benediction of the Name flows through the dhāma and the previous ācāryas. After pilgrimage, internalize the realization rather than leaving it behind — absorb the sacred atmosphere into the heart and carry it into ordinary life.

## Glossary

- **tapas** — Austerity or penance; the personal dedication and discipline by which a Vaiṣṇava keeps a holy place alive and accessible.
- **tīrtha** — A holy place of pilgrimage; here, Ṭer Kadamba as a cultivated garden made sacred by devotional service.
- **Ṭer Kadamba** — The Vraja site where Kṛṣṇa grazed and called the cows and gopīs and climbed the kadamba tree; 'ṭer' means 'to call.' Rūpa Gosvāmī sat here to write.
- **kīrtana / saṅkīrtana** — Congregational singing of the holy name; the uninterrupted 'all-time bhakti' atmosphere of Vraja and the means of cleansing the heart.
- **līlā** — The transcendental pastimes of Kṛṣṇa, which double as instruction for the devotee.
- **mākhan / man** — Butter / mind — the wordplay that the 'butter' Kṛṣṇa really wants is the devotee's mind, cleansed by chanting.
- **mahā-mantra** — The great chant (Hare Kṛṣṇa); the holy name, non-different from Kṛṣṇa Himself.
- **vigraha** — The Deity form of the Lord; non-different from the Name and from Bhagavān.
- **Bhagavān** — The Supreme Personality of Godhead, Kṛṣṇa Himself.
- **cid-ānanda** — Fully spiritual — composed of consciousness (cit) and bliss (ānanda); the nature shared by the Name, the Deity, and Bhagavān.
- **uttamā bhakti** — Pure devotion as defined by Rūpa Gosvāmī: favorable service to Kṛṣṇa free of ulterior desire and uncovered by fruitive action (karma) or speculative knowledge (jñāna).
- **Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu** — Rūpa Gosvāmī's foundational treatise on devotional rasa, composed at Ṭer Kadamba, which opens with the definition of uttamā bhakti.
- **Rūpānuga** — A Vaiṣṇava who follows in the footsteps of Śrīla Rūpa Gosvāmī — a line of succession in devotional mood, not merely doctrine.
- **kṣīra / kheer** — Sweet rice; in the pastime, provided through Rādhārāṇī's grace for Rūpa Gosvāmī's wish to feed a Vaiṣṇava — receiving it is receiving Her mercy.
- **Sva-niyama-daśakam** — Raghunātha Dāsa Gosvāmī's ten personal vows, modeling the resolve of a serious sādhaka — to love, to reside in Vraja, to surrender, and to serve Rādhā.
- **Gāyatrī** — The sacred mantra received from the spiritual master, named in Raghunātha's first vow as something to be loved, not merely recited.
- **sādhaka** — A practitioner engaged in spiritual discipline (sādhana).
- **Vraja / Vṛndāvana** — Kṛṣṇa's intimate land of pastimes, held supremely dear — preferred above Vaikuṇṭha and Dvārakā — because Rādhā is there.
- **Vaikuṇṭha / Dvārakā** — Realms of the Lord's majesty and opulence, set in contrast to the intimacy of Vraja.
- **rādhā-dāsya** — Exclusive servitude to Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī; honoring those who worship Her and shunning those who neglect Her while worshiping Govinda.
- **bhajana** — Intimate worship and devotional practice; here, the helpless, renounced worship of the Divine Couple at Rādhā-kuṇḍa.
- **Rādhā-kuṇḍa** — The sacred pond most dear to Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa; the place of renounced bhajana in the company of Rūpa and Jīva Gosvāmīs.
- **phala-śruti** — The concluding statement of the fruit or benediction of a text — here, that whoever reads or hears the vows with fixed mind attains Vraja.
- **japa** — Soft, individual chanting of the mantra on beads, deepened by mentally residing in the dhāma.
- **dhāma** — The holy abode; the sacred place whose benediction flows through it, to be carried in the heart and absorbed even after leaving it physically.
