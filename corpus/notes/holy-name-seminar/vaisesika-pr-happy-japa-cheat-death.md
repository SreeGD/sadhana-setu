# Vaisesika Pr Happy Japa Cheat Death

## Theme

Drawing on Bhagavad-gītā 8.6, the lecture builds a tight argument about consciousness at the moment of death: whatever state of being (bhāva) one remembers when leaving the body determines one's next destination, and this is not optional — we are forced to go where the mind goes. The speaker clarifies that bhāva here means emotion, specifically love of Krishna: the predominant emotion cultivated during life, and present at death, sets where we go next, making this lifetime the opportunity to establish Krishna consciousness as one's ruling emotion. The practical guarantee offered is daily chanting of Hare Krishna in set-aside time, excluding all other sounds and thoughts, while praying to the Lord and remembering His promise of deliverance from the material world — a practice said to assure easy remembrance of Krishna at death and the complete success of life. The lecture crystallizes this in the phrase 'cheat death before death cheats you': real intelligence is using japa now to prepare for the moment of death rather than letting death arrive unprepared.

## Practical application

Set aside a dedicated time each day to chant Hare Krishna, deliberately excluding all other sounds and thoughts during that period. Within the chanting, simply pray to the Lord and consciously remember His promise to deliver you from the material world. Treat this daily japa not as routine but as rehearsal for the moment of death — since the emotion predominant at death determines the next destination, use each session to cultivate love of Krishna as your ruling emotion now, so that remembrance of Him at death comes easily. Approach the practice with the urgency of the lecture's motto: prepare through japa today rather than letting death find you unprepared.

## Glossary

- **bhāva** — State of being or emotion; in Bhagavad-gītā 8.6, the state one remembers at death that determines the next destination — explained in the lecture as the predominant emotion, ideally love of Krishna.
- **Bhagavad-gītā 8.6** — Verse teaching that whatever state of being one remembers when quitting the body, that state one attains without fail.
- **japa** — Private, meditative chanting of the Lord's names — here, the daily practice of chanting Hare Krishna that prepares one for remembrance of Krishna at death.
- **Hare Krishna** — The mahā-mantra chanted as the core daily practice; chanting it while excluding other sounds and thoughts assures remembrance of Krishna at death.
- **Krishna consciousness** — Absorption in remembrance and love of Krishna, cultivated during life so that it becomes the ruling emotion present at the moment of death.
