# Contract: Transformation Design-Review Rubric (FR-011)

How the redesign is judged to "bring transformation" **without measuring or scoring the devotee**
(Constitution IV). Applied at review time against a rendered day's reading (and a sample across
several days). Pass = every item checked.

## Arc presence

- [ ] **Orient** — leads with a contemplative orientation toward attentive chanting (the
      chanter's disposition + the glory of the Name), not an information list (FR-001).
- [ ] **Deepen** — surfaces one Hari-Nāma teaching that is **citation-bearing and KG-grounded**
      (or a clearly-cited curated fallback) (FR-003/004).
- [ ] **Apply** — offers exactly one optional contemplative micro-practice that requires no input
      (FR-005).
- [ ] **Enter japa** — closes by pointing *into* japa with a resolve/prayer, not into more
      reading (FR-002); optional sankalpa echo present when a check-in exists (FR-012).

## Discipline

- [ ] Reading completes within ~60–75 seconds (FR-009 / SC-001).
- [ ] Stable within a day; rotates across days (FR-007).
- [ ] Renders with graceful fallback whether or not kg-mcp is available; no broken/empty state
      (FR-008 / SC-004).

## Sattvic-medium audit (same bar as the v1 audit — SC-003)

- [ ] No streaks, badges, points, or gamification.
- [ ] No quantifying, scoring, or judging the devotee's chanting.
- [ ] No push notifications.
- [ ] No screen interaction expected during japa (the reading is *before*).
- [ ] The micro-practice records nothing and is skippable.

## Provenance (SC-002)

- [ ] 100% of Sanskrit/verse/teaching content shown is citation-bearing and KG-grounded; nothing
      fabricated.
