## A Note on Sources & Method  *(please read)*

This book follows the paramparā principle: its teachings are grounded in śāstra (revealed scripture)
and in the commentaries of the ācāryas — not in personal speculation. Two honest clarifications
belong here, so the reader knows exactly what they are holding:

1. **The diagnostic framework is a teaching device, not a single named scripture.** The organizing
   chain of this book —
   > *Guṇas → Ṣaḍ-Ripu (six enemies) → Anarthas → Pāpa → Aparādha → the Blocked Name*
   — is a **pedagogical synthesis** assembled by the compiler from teachings that are individually
   scriptural. Each *link* is traditional; the *sequence and the clinical framing* are an editorial
   aid to understanding, offered as a lens, not presented as a verse or a fixed śāstric list. Where
   the book says "no speculation," it means every *claim* is sourced — not that the *arrangement* is
   itself revealed.

2. **Two kinds of quotation, always distinguished.**
   - *Verbatim* — Sanskrit verses and their English renderings are quoted exactly and cited to book,
     canto/chapter, and verse.
   - *Paraphrase* — where a teaching is summarized in the compiler's words (marked "(paraphrased)" or
     "(as taught in …)"), it is a faithful summary, **not** a verbatim quotation. These may be
     replaced with exact wording in a later edition.
