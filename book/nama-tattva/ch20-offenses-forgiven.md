# Chapter 20 · Offenses Forgiven

> *"Abandon all varieties of religion and just surrender unto Me. I shall deliver you from all sinful reactions. Do not fear."*
> — **Kṛṣṇa, Bhagavad-gītā 18.66**

---

Sins are one thing; **offenses to the name** (Chapter 7) are subtler and more obstinate — they are
the last clouds to lift. This chapter is about their forgiveness, and it is the tenderest turn in the
whole book, because it answers the fear left by Chapter 3: *"After all my offenses, is it too late for
me?"* Kṛṣṇa's own answer is *mā śucaḥ* — "do not fear."

## 20.1 How Offenses Are Forgiven

| Offense | How it clears | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| to a devotee | serve and satisfy the offended one | until they are pleased |
| to the guru | deepen submission and service | by the guru's mercy |
| to scripture | study with faith and humility | gradual |
| to the name itself | keep chanting despite feeling unworthy | by the name's own mercy |

## 20.2 Seven Steps to Forgiveness

| Step | Inner movement |
|---|---|
| 1 Recognize | "I committed this offense." |
| 2 Feel remorse | genuine regret (not mere fear of consequence) |
| 3 Confess | to a senior devotee, where appropriate |
| 4 Apologize | sincerely, to the one offended |
| 5 Resolve | "I will not repeat this." |
| 6 Serve | render specific service to the offended party |
| 7 Chant | with renewed attention and humility |

## 20.3 The Ground of All Forgiveness

BG 18.66 is Kṛṣṇa's personal promise: to one who fully surrenders, even offenses are dissolved by His
mercy — not by the offender's merit. Surrender is not a technique that *earns* forgiveness; it is the
open hand into which forgiveness is placed. The name that seemed silent for fifteen years (Chapter 3)
was never withholding forgiveness — only waiting for the heart to turn toward it.

> **From the lectures.** Sacīnandana Swami Mahārāja teaches this at Surabhi Kuṇḍa — the very place
> where Indra, after grievously offending Vraja, was forgiven — showing that forgiveness of offenses
> is precisely what allows the holy name to at last bring Kṛṣṇa near.
