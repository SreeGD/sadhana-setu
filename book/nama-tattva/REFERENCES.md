# References & Sources

*Nāma-Tattva: Why My Chanting Isn't Working (and How to Fix It)*

This edition follows the paramparā method: **no speculation — only śāstra and the ācāryas.** Verse
transliterations and English translations follow the standard Bhaktivedanta Book Trust editions as
published on **[Vedabase](https://vedabase.io)**. Every scriptural citation below was checked against
Vedabase / Vāṇisource during a dedicated verification pass; the status column records the result.

## 3. Contemporary lecture sources (the corpus)

The "From the lectures" panels and the contemporary-teacher epigraphs draw on a corpus of recorded
Holy-Name lectures by three ISKCON teachers, sourced from **ISKCON Desire Tree**
(audio.iskcondesiretree.com), transcribed with OpenAI Whisper (large-v3-turbo) and enriched into
study notes.

**Transcripts & enriched notes (open repository):**
> **https://github.com/SreeGD/sadhana-setu** — see `corpus/transcripts/` (verbatim transcripts) and
> `corpus/notes/` (enriched notes).

**Provenance of contemporary quotations.** Direct quotations attributed to a contemporary teacher
(e.g., Bhūrijana Prabhu, "Be determined to hear one mantra"; Vaiśeṣika Prabhu, "Bring your mind back
to the mantra itself") are **verbatim lines from the linked transcripts**. Statements marked
"(as taught in …)" summarize a teaching from the named lecture rather than quoting it word-for-word.
The full note→chapter mapping is in **TOC.md, Appendix J**.
