# Chapter 8 · Nāmābhāsa — The Blocked Holy Name (Result)

> *Chanting matures through three stages — the offensive name, the shadow of the name (nāmābhāsa), and at last the pure name.*
> — the teaching of **Śrīla Haridāsa Ṭhākura**, *Harināma Cintāmaṇi* (paraphrased)

---

The chain of Part Two ends here, at its **result**: the blocked name — chanting that touches the lips
but not the heart. But "blocked" is not a dead end; it is a *stage on a road*. Understanding the
three stages of chanting turns a discouraging diagnosis into a map with a clear direction of travel.

## 8.1 The Three Stages (Hari-nāma-cintāmaṇi)

| Stage | Sanskrit | What it is | Felt reality |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Nāmāparādha** | offensive chanting | mind wanders; dryness; duty |
| 2 | **Nāmābhāsa** | the *shadow* of the name | flickers of taste; some sweetness |
| 3 | **Śuddha-nāma** | the *pure* name | steady taste; the name as a Person; prema |

The movement is not automatic with quantity; it is unlocked by **clearing offenses** (Chapter 7) and
by *quality* of attention. One does not chant one's way from Stage 1 to Stage 3 by speed — one clears
clouds, and the same rounds begin to shine.

## 8.2 The Astonishing Power of the Shadow

Recall SB 6.2.14 (Chapter 2): even *nāmābhāsa* — the mere shadow — destroys unlimited sins and
delivered Ajāmila. This is the great encouragement for the blocked chanter: **even your dim chanting
is already working.** Like ultraviolet light through cloud, it acts whether or not you feel it. The
task is not to manufacture feeling but to keep chanting while thinning the cloud.

## 8.3 The One Disqualification

There is a single posture that blocks even the subtle rays: the *Māyāvāda* idea that "I am becoming
God by chanting." Offenseless chanting is sub-servient and prayerful — a helpless soul calling the
Lord (Chapters 10–13). The moment chanting is turned into a technique for self-elevation-into-God, it
inverts its own direction.

> **From the lectures.** Sacīnandana Swami Mahārāja maps the same ascent — from "shadow chanting" up
> into "the light of pure devotion" — again and again across the Kirtan Course (sessions 07 and 10):
> the name reveals itself by *its own mercy*, not by our technique; our part is attentive, humble
> persistence while the offenses lift.

## 8.4 The Hinge

Part Two has now shown the whole disease: **why** the enemies arise (Ch 4), the **residue** they leave
(Ch 5), the **sins** they branch into (Ch 6), the **offenses** that curtain the name (Ch 7), and the
**blocked chanting** that results (Ch 8). The general anatomy is complete.

But a disease described is not a patient diagnosed. The next chapter turns all of this inward: not
"here is the chain" but **"here is exactly where the chain binds *me*, and what to do today."**
