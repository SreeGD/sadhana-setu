# Chapter 16 · Japa Quick-Reference Cards

> *"Call to Him — Kṛṣṇa, Kṛṣṇa — and hear the mantra."*
> — **HG Bhūrijana Prabhu** (Japa Retreat)

---

Everything in Part Three condenses here into **portable tools** — cards to keep by the japa seat. This
chapter is the field manual; the full seventeen cards are printed in Appendix reference form. Below
is the core set and how to use them.

## 16.1 The Four Foundation Cards

**Card 1 — Three-Step Meditation** *(Sacīnandana Swami Mahārāja)*: before japa, establish (1) *who I
am* — "I am a part of Kṛṣṇa, His eternal servant"; (2) *who Kṛṣṇa is* — "my only master"; (3) *the
purpose of this world* — to reform my forgetfulness. Then set the intention: *"I am not chanting to
finish rounds; I am calling out — please accept me."*

**Card 2 — The Two Doors** *(Sacīnandana Swami Mahārāja)*: before each mantra, choose. **Door 1 —
mechanical:** pronunciation only, "finishing rounds." **Door 2 — with love:** calling to Kṛṣṇa. Door 1
frees from hell; **Door 2 draws Kṛṣṇa's notice.** Consciously choose Door 2.

**Card 3 — Bhūrijana Prabhu's 4-Step Method:**

| Step | Practice |
|---|---|
| 1 Saṅkalpa | "I will hear just *this one* mantra." (a request the mind can obey) |
| 2 Neglect the mind | when it rebels, ignore it — like a stern parent with a whining child |
| 3 Take shelter | the Name *is* Kṛṣṇa; take shelter in the sound |
| 4 Await mercy | mercy is *received*, not achieved — chant attentively and wait |

**Card 4 — Pre-Japa Checklist:** body settled, phone away, obeisances offered, one clear saṅkalpa,
heart forgiven of anyone, mood of *servant* not *demanding controller*.

## 16.2 The Deeper Preparation

**Card 5 — Govardhana Pañca-Bhūta Śuddhi** (15 min): a five-element settling of body and breath —
earth (grounding), water (flowing movement), fire (bhastrikā), air (nāḍī-śodhana), ether (three
saṅkalpas) — so that, per SB 8.3.1, the mind can rest *in the heart* before the first bead.

## 16.3 Troubleshooting (quick fixes)

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| mind won't focus | chant louder, slower; hear each syllable |
| sleepy | stand, walk, wash the face, chant aloud |
| racing thoughts | note them on paper; return to the Name |
| mechanical/dry | add the prayer: "please reveal Yourself" |
| rushing | set 7–8 min per round; slow the first round deliberately |

**Golden rule:** when the mind wanders, do not fight — gently *return*.
